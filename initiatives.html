Our initiatives

Creating space
to flourish.

We bring health, sport, creativity and community together to make participation more accessible.

Our focus areas

Different pathways.
One shared purpose.

Our initiatives are designed to complement one another — supporting wellbeing while creating more ways for young people to participate.

01

Health & dignity

Menstrual health

We aim to support menstrual health awareness and improve access to essential period products, particularly for girls who may face barriers to reliable information and resources.

  • Menstrual health awareness and education
  • Fundraising for essential period products
  • Community partnerships and distribution initiatives
02

Movement & confidence

Sport opportunities

Sport can create space for connection, confidence and belonging. We support events and partnerships that invite more young people to participate, regardless of their experience level.

  • Community sporting events and tournaments
  • Inclusive opportunities to participate
  • Fundraising through sport-led experiences
03

Expression & imagination

Creative opportunities

Creative experiences help young people explore ideas, build skills and share their perspectives. We aim to connect young people with accessible ways to create and express themselves.

  • Creative workshops and showcases
  • Artistic events and collaborative projects
  • Platforms for young voices and ideas
04

People & partnership

Community action

Lasting initiatives depend on listening and working together. We seek partnerships with schools, organisations and local communities to shape projects around shared priorities.

  • Volunteer-led projects
  • School and organisation partnerships
  • Community outreach and engagement
