About Girls United Vietnam

We believe in
her possibility.

Building a community where girls and young people have more opportunities to learn, move, create and thrive.

Our mission

Opportunity should never be out of reach.

Girls United Vietnam works to expand opportunities for girls and young people through menstrual health awareness, sport, creativity and community engagement.

We bring people together to turn shared interests into collective action — creating experiences that build confidence, encourage participation and support communities.

Our belief

Every young person deserves the chance to take part.

01

Dignity

We believe access to menstrual health information and essential products is part of dignity and wellbeing.

02

Opportunity

We want more young people to access sport, creativity and experiences that help them grow.

03

Community

We believe progress is stronger when young people, organisations and communities work together.

How we work

Small actions.
Shared momentum.

Our work is designed around collaboration. We aim to partner with schools, community organisations, event organisers and supporters to create initiatives that respond to real needs.

We are building our programmes step by step, listening to communities and learning from the people our work is intended to support.
