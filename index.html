Who we are

Every girl deserves the freedom to participate.

Girls United Vietnam is a youth-led initiative working to make opportunity more accessible to girls and young people across Vietnam.

We bring people together through community events, creative experiences and sport — while supporting menstrual health awareness and access to essential products.
